bx leak check

Two halves of the evidence: what your browser reveals, and what bx sees on this machine. Neither half alone can tell you whether traffic is leaking.

Before anything is sent: this page will contact the following third parties from your browser. They will see your real exit address.

  • IPv4 echo: {{.Endpoints.EchoV4}}
  • IPv6 echo: {{.Endpoints.EchoV6}}
  • STUN (WebRTC): {{.Endpoints.STUN}}
  • Exit country: {{.Endpoints.Trace}}

Nothing is stored. bx keeps no history of this check.
