bx leak check

Two halves of the evidence: what your browser reveals, and what bx sees on this machine. Neither half alone can tell you whether traffic is leaking.

Before this page sends anything: it will contact the following third parties from your browser. They will see your real exit address.

  • IPv4 echo {{.Endpoints.EchoV4}}
  • IPv6 echo {{.Endpoints.EchoV6}}
  • STUN (WebRTC) {{.Endpoints.STUN}}
  • Exit country {{.Endpoints.Trace}}

This page is only half of the check, and the other half has already run. Unless you passed --no-reach, bx probed the AI service endpoints from this machine over your current network path before opening this page, and listed those addresses in the terminal.

Nothing is stored. bx keeps no history of this check.
